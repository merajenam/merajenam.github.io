Let's Tap In · IntegrationLead capture pipeline: from landing page to follow-up

Shipped

Problem

Landing page leads had no single home. Form submissions had to reach the team's workflow tool before anyone could track or follow up, and there was no clear view of how visitors moved through the funnel.

What I did

  • Designed and launched 5 lead-gen landing pages using AI-assisted development with Claude.
  • Set up Meta Pixel events to capture on-page behavior and conversions for funnel analysis.

03

Let's Tap In · Analytics + AIA performance dashboard that tells you what to post next

In progress

Problem

Engagement numbers without context don't tell you what to change. Raw view counts reward big accounts, and paid boosts muddy the lessons.

What I'm building

  • A Meta API dashboard with Overview, Ads, Organic and Reports views across 7, 14, 30 and 90-day windows.
  • An Ideas engine that scans a benchmark set of 26 agency accounts and surfaces reels that beat their own typical views by 2× or more.
  • Ranks the winners by views per follower, so account size doesn't matter, and filters out reels that look promoted.
  • Uses Claude to turn each winning reel into a concept, hook and format recommendation. It refreshes weekly.

Stack

Meta Graph APINext.jsClaude APIBenchmarkingOutlier detection

Goal

Replace the weekly "what happened?" report with a weekly "here's what to try next" brief, based on what's working for comparable accounts.

Why I do this

The best marketing isn't selling. It's making someone feel the problem the way you do, then proving it worked.

In Bangladesh, primary school was free, but kids kept dropping out because their families needed the income. The gap between a child working and a child learning was about fifteen dollars a month.

AlterYouth closed that gap one subscription at a time. Building it taught me how communities grow, how stories move people to act, and how to read the data to see what's actually driving growth. I've brought that mix of storytelling, systems and measurement to every role since.
